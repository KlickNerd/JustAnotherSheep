Arriving is easy. That is the part everybody remembers.

04 / Meet Sheep

Just Another
Sheep.

At least, that is what they called him.

Sheep grew up like everyone else on the farm: follow the bell, stay inside the lines and never ask too many questions.

For a while, that was comfortable enough. But Sheep has always noticed the little things. The locked gates. The watchful speakers. How every day looks suspiciously like the one before it.

He is not a hero. He does not have a master plan. He simply believes a life without choices is not much of a life.

So while the flock follows the schedule, Sheep has started writing things on walls.

Apparently, independent thinking is a personality defect.

06 / The Open Gate

Truth travels hoof to hoof.

Vol. 01 · Farm edition · Printed before the morning bell

Sheep is not the only one on the farm building something after hours.

Ewe listens. She asks precise questions, writes the answers down and notices the details the flock has learned to overlook.

At night she sets them in type. The Open Gate is an independent paper: she writes it, prints it and carries it across the farm before the bell rings.

It does not tell the flock what to think. It tells them what happened and trusts them with the rest.

07 / The other side

The fence was never the horizon.

Sheep had spent his whole life being told what waited beyond the farm.

Uncertainty. Disorder. Mistakes without anyone there to correct them.

But when he looked toward Solangeles, he saw something the farm had never offered him.

A choice.

The city promised no safety and no simple answers. It only proved that another way of living was possible.

For the first time, the unknown did not feel empty.

It felt open.

The gate was still closed.
Sheep was no longer certain that it should be.

The farm is still watching.

The next bell won't sound the same.

The fences still stand. The flock still follows. The farm still expects tomorrow to look exactly like today.

But one sheep has stopped waiting for permission.

He started making noise. Others have started listening.

Tomorrow morning, the flock will hear it too.
