02 / Life in the flock

A perfect day. Every day.

The farm provides food, shelter and protection. Nobody gets lost. Nobody is left behind. Nobody has to worry about making the wrong choice.

Comfort is easy when every decision has already been made for you.

Please enjoy your freedom between the fences.

Ewe writes down what actually happened. She listens, checks twice, and notices the details the flock has learned to overlook. She prints at night — and trusts the flock with the rest.

06 / The other side

The fence was never the horizon.

Posted at the gate

Beyond this point: uncertainty. Disorder. Mistakes without supervision.

…and the lights nobody mentions.

Nobody on the farm talks about the lights behind the hills. That is how every sheep knows they are real.

The city out there promises nothing. No safety, no schedule, no shepherd. It only proves that another way of living exists.

The gate was still closed.
Sheep was no longer certain that it should be.

“I’m not lost. I just stopped following.”— found written on the fence post

Final announcement of the day

The farm is still watching.

The bell will ring again tomorrow at seven. The flock will answer.

But some nights there is a different rhythm coming from the west barn. And the herd that hears it is growing.

The next bell won’t sound the same.
