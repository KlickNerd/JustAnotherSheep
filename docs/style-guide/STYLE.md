# Just Another Sheep — Visual Style Guide

Canonical art direction for every generated image. Treat the two reference
images in this folder as binding; any new artwork must match them.

## The rule

**Painted world, inked residents.**

- **Environments without characters** (hero panoramas, establishing shots):
  painted illustration in the style of `ref-02-peacehold-painted.jpg` —
  soft painterly rendering, warm muted light, symmetric "a little too
  perfect" composition. Recurring landmarks: central bell tower with a red
  beacon light, white wooden barns, loudspeakers on gables and poles,
  white fences, rolling hills.
- **Every scene with characters**: detailed adult comic illustration in the
  style of `ref-01-sheep-waking.webp` — bold clean dark ink outlines, rich
  hatched/stippled texture, muted earthy palette, naturalistic cinematic
  light, expressive faces. No photorealism, no plush/felt-puppet look, no
  children's-book softness.
- **The realistic (photoreal) Sheep is reserved.** He does not appear on
  the website until the planned community reveal (music video / release
  moment).

## Black Sheep — character canon

**Primary reference: the five-image character sheet (`sheet-01` … `sheet-05`).**
Every new image of Sheep must match it. `ref-01-sheep-waking.webp` remains a
style sample for scene rendering, not the character authority.

- Anthropomorphic black sheep, young adult male, lean build.
- Round curly charcoal-black wool cap with a wool mane down the neck,
  droopy ears, flat muzzle, **warm brown eyes**, tired half-lidded gaze.
- **Default expression: deadpan** (`sheet-05`). The faint smile
  (`sheet-04`) is the exception for special moments.
- Wardrobe (Farm Era): plain dark grey t-shirt, **brown work trousers**,
  worn dark boots. Deliberately plain — "no unnecessary iconisation".
- **No chain necklace in the Farm Era.** The chain with the angular
  pendant is reserved: it appears later as a visible marker of Sheep's
  next era, like the realistic Sheep reveal. Sunglasses are a rare prop,
  not part of the daily look.
- Attitude: calm, observant, never agitated. The joke lives in his face,
  not in his gestures.

## World details worth keeping consistent

- Loudspeakers everywhere: wall-mounted boxes indoors, horn clusters on
  poles outdoors.
- The bell tower (with red beacon) is Peacehold's visual anchor.
- Signage and notices exist in the world, but **generated images carry no
  readable text** — typography is added in HTML, never baked into pixels.

## Prompt base (copy into every character generation)

> Detailed adult comic illustration, bold clean dark ink outlines, rich
> hatched texture, muted earthy palette, naturalistic cinematic light.
> Same character and exact same rendering style as the character sheet:
> anthropomorphic black sheep, round curly charcoal wool, wool mane down
> the neck, droopy ears, warm brown tired half-lidded eyes, deadpan
> expression, plain dark grey t-shirt, brown work trousers, worn boots.
> No chain, no sunglasses. No photorealism, no plush or felt look,
> no readable text.

## Production pipeline (agreed 2026-10-14)

- **Character artwork** (anything showing Sheep or other residents) is
  generated by the team in ChatGPT, where the character's look is
  established. Finished images are handed over via chat or committed to
  this repo; Claude handles cropping, responsive web variants,
  integration and a consistency check against this guide.
- **Environments and backdrops** (no characters) may be generated via
  Higgsfield, but only against the references in this folder and only
  after sign-off on a single test image.
- Nothing generated goes onto the website without explicit approval.
