# Just Another Sheep — Visual Style Guide

Canonical art direction for every generated image. Treat the two reference
images in this folder as binding; any new artwork must match them.

## The rule

**Painted world, inked residents.**

- **Environments without characters** (hero panoramas, establishing shots):
  painted illustration in the style of `ref-02-peacehold-painted.jpg` —
  soft painterly rendering, warm muted light, symmetric "a little too
  perfect" composition. Recurring landmarks: central bell tower with a red
  beacon light, white wooden barns, loudspeakers on gables and poles,
  white fences, rolling hills.
- **Every scene with characters**: detailed adult comic illustration in the
  style of `ref-01-sheep-waking.webp` — bold clean dark ink outlines, rich
  hatched/stippled texture, muted earthy palette, naturalistic cinematic
  light, expressive faces. No photorealism, no plush/felt-puppet look, no
  children's-book softness.
- **The realistic (photoreal) Sheep is reserved.** He does not appear on
  the website until the planned community reveal (music video / release
  moment).

## Black Sheep — character canon

- Anthropomorphic black sheep, young adult male, lean build.
- Curly charcoal-black wool, droopy ears, tired half-lidded eyes,
  dry unimpressed expression as the default.
- Wardrobe: plain dark t-shirt, dark trousers, worn boots.
- Props: chain necklace with a small angular pendant; black sunglasses
  (usually resting somewhere nearby, worn only for effect).
- Attitude: calm, observant, never agitated. The joke lives in his face,
  not in his gestures.

## World details worth keeping consistent

- Loudspeakers everywhere: wall-mounted boxes indoors, horn clusters on
  poles outdoors.
- The bell tower (with red beacon) is Peacehold's visual anchor.
- Signage and notices exist in the world, but **generated images carry no
  readable text** — typography is added in HTML, never baked into pixels.
