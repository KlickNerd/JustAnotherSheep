# 07 · HOOF TO HOOF

*Akt II — Merken. Ewes Track. Er ist nicht allein.
Sie führt, er antwortet. Sie kämpft nicht mit Wut, sondern mit Genauigkeit.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Hoof to Hoof
```

**Style of Music**
```
Warm acoustic duet, 88 BPM, minor key. Female lead vocal, clear, steady,
precise, unsentimental. Male second voice answering, lower and looser.
Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, upright bass, brushed drums. Percussion built
from a hand printing press: paper, rollers, metal levers, a small bell.
Intimate, nocturnal, patient. Folk with a rhythmic backbone.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
power ballad, orchestral swell, autotune, EDM, aggressive rap, breathy pop
vocal, romantic love duet, gospel choir
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1 - Ewe]
He writes on walls. I write it down.
Same thing, slower.
He gets a night, I get a page,
and his washes off by Sunday.

I don't need it to be loud.
I need it to be right.
Ask a question twice and write the answer
either way it comes.

[Verse 2 - Sheep]
She checks the thing I already said
and then she checks it again.
I make the noise so they look up.
She's already at the desk.

[Chorus - both]
Hoof to hoof and folded twice,
under the door before the light.
They can paint over mine.
They'd have to burn eight hundred of hers.
Truth travels hoof to hoof.

[Verse 3 - Ewe]
I don't tell them what to think,
that's the part they can't forgive.
Tell a sheep a thing that happened
and it's theirs. You don't get it back.

They can call it troublemaking.
They can call it what they like.
It's just the feeding times, the numbers,
and what somebody actually said.

[Chorus - both]
Hoof to hoof and folded twice,
under the door before the light.
They can paint over mine.
They'd have to burn eight hundred of hers.
Truth travels hoof to hoof.

[Bridge - spoken, Ewe, no music]
Correction, page four.
Last week we reported the east gate was locked at nine.
It was locked at seven.
We regret the error.
We do not regret the question.

[Final Chorus - both, fuller, press rhythm louder]
Hoof to hoof and folded twice,
under the door before the light.
Some stories travel better folded.
Truth travels hoof to hoof.

[Outro - press, then one bell strike]
```

---

## Notizen

* Die **gesprochene Korrektur** in der Bridge ist der beste Moment des Tracks:
  Eine Zeitungsberichtigung als Songbridge. Trocken vortragen, ohne Musik.
  „We do not regret the question" ist Ewes ganze Figur in einem Satz.
* Ewe ist **nicht** die romantische Verbündete. Kein Liebeslied. Zwei Leute,
  die dieselbe Arbeit unterschiedlich machen.
* „Truth travels hoof to hoof" und „Some stories travel better folded" stehen
  wörtlich auf der Website — bewusste Klammer.
* Bei Suno bekommt man selten zwei saubere Stimmen. Notfalls zwei Takes
  generieren (einmal weibliche Lead, einmal männliche) und die Verse in der
  DAW verschränken.
