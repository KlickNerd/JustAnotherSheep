# 10 · VOLUNTEERS

*Akt III — Druck. Der härteste Track des Albums.
Nicht die Wächter melden dich — die Herde tut es. Das System ist nicht draußen,
es ist die Gewohnheit aller. Inhaltlich die Mitte des ganzen Projekts.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Volunteers
```

**Style of Music**
```
Dark choral folk, 80 BPM, minor key. Large mixed choir singing beautifully in
unison, hand drum, low drone, sparse acoustic guitar, no kit drums. Solo male
voice alternating with the choir. Church-like space, wide reverb on the choir,
dry and close on the solo voice. Solemn, hymn-like, and quietly horrifying
because it is so lovely.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
aggressive, metal, distorted, EDM, trap, cheerful, pop chorus, autotune,
horror soundtrack, screaming
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1 - solo voice, almost spoken]
They didn't send the dog for me.
They didn't need to.
They asked the room a question
and the room answered.

[Chorus - full choir, beautiful]
Volunteers. Volunteers.
They asked, and the hooves went up.
Nobody made us. Nobody made us.
That's the part I can't get past.

[Verse 2 - solo]
Martha at the trough. She's kind.
She held my head the winter I was sick.
She raised her hoof so fast she didn't think,
and she is kind. Both things are true.

Tom, who taught me where the good grass is.
Ada, who has never hurt a thing.
Not one of them a monster.
Not one of them said no.

[Chorus - choir]
Volunteers. Volunteers.
They asked, and the hooves went up.
Nobody made us. Nobody made us.
That's the part I can't get past.

[Bridge - drone, one voice, no choir]
The fence was never the thing that held us.
The fence is wood. The fence is old.
You could walk around the fence in an afternoon
if nobody was watching.

Somebody is always watching.
Not because they're told to.
Because it's Tuesday, and that's what we do.
We were the thing that held us.

[Final Chorus - choir enters slowly, builds, then falls away voice by voice]
Volunteers. Volunteers.
They asked, and the hooves went up.
Nobody made us. Nobody made us.
Nobody made us.

[Outro - one voice left, no music]
Nobody made us.
```

---

## Notizen

* Der Chor muss **schön** klingen. Kein Horror-Sounddesign, keine dissonanten
  Streicher. Die Bedrohung ist, dass es klingt wie ein Kirchenlied.
* „and she is kind. Both things are true." — die entscheidende Zeile. Keine
  Verurteilung. Martha ist nicht böse geworden, sie war immer so.
* Martha kommt aus Track 09. Wer aufmerksam hört, merkt: Ihre Frage dort war
  keine Fürsorge.
* Der Schluss löst sich Stimme für Stimme auf, bis eine übrig bleibt — dieselbe
  Bewegung wie beim Headcount in Track 03, nur rückwärts.
