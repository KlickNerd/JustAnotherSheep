# 09 · CURFEW

*Akt III — Druck. Sie haben ihn bemerkt.
Die Farm wird nicht härter, sie wird aufmerksamer. Nichts Schlimmes passiert —
und genau das ist unerträglich.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Curfew
```

**Style of Music**
```
Tense minimal post-punk, 96 BPM, minor key. Dry tight drums, one low pulsing
note, muted guitar stabs, almost no bass, lots of empty space. Male vocal
close, restrained, slightly clipped, no vibrato. Reversed tannoy announcements
buried in the background. Cold room, claustrophobic, patient. Nothing resolves.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
uplifting, anthem, big chorus, orchestral, EDM drop, distorted rock, warm
strings, gospel, cheerful, wide reverb
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1]
Nothing has changed.
They just put a light by the west barn door.
Nothing has changed.
They just moved the evening count an hour.

Nothing has changed.
New man at the gate, and he's friendly,
learns your number in a day,
asks how you slept and writes it down.

[Chorus]
Nothing has changed, they keep saying,
and they're right, and that's the problem.
Nothing has changed
except when I can walk.

[Verse 2]
Nobody said a word about the paint.
Nobody has said a word about the paint.
Nobody looks at me differently.
Nobody looks at me at all.

Supper at the usual hour.
Same seat. Same bowl. Same everyone.
Martha asked if I'd been sleeping.
Martha's never asked me that.

[Chorus]
Nothing has changed, they keep saying,
and they're right, and that's the problem.
Nothing has changed
except when I can walk.

[Bridge - drums drop, one note, spoken]
It isn't fear. Fear would be simpler.
It's the feeling of a room
where somebody has already been
and put everything back exactly.

[Final Chorus - thinner, one voice]
Nothing has changed.
Nothing has changed.
Nothing has changed.

[Outro - tannoy, warm and kind]
For your comfort, the lights will now stay on.
```

---

## Notizen

* Der Track darf **nicht** dramatisch werden. Keine Steigerung, kein Ausbruch.
  Die Spannung entsteht daraus, dass nichts passiert.
* „Martha's never asked me that" — die einzige Zeile, die verrät, dass er
  beobachtet wird. Beiläufig singen, nicht betonen. Martha kommt in Track 10
  wieder.
* Der Durchsagesatz am Ende ist die Pointe: Fürsorge als Überwachung.
  Freundlich sprechen lassen, im selben Ton wie in Track 01.
* Wenn das Album auf zwölf Tracks gekürzt wird, ist das einer der beiden
  Kandidaten zum Streichen (Funktion überschneidet sich mit Track 10).
