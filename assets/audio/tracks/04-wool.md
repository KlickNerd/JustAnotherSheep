# 04 · WOOL

*Akt I — Drinnen. Was sie nehmen, während du dich wohlfühlst.
Der erste echte Riss. Kein Zorn — Müdigkeit.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Wool
```

**Style of Music**
```
Slow folk blues, 72 BPM, minor key, heavy and unhurried. Resonator slide
guitar, upright bass, brushed snare, faint harmonium. Weary low male voice,
close and intimate, almost talking. Lots of room, tape warmth, dust. Southern
gothic without theatrics. Restrained, sad, resigned rather than angry.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
uplifting, pop chorus, drums build, EDM, orchestral swell, angry vocals,
shouting, distortion, fast tempo
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1]
They keep the barn warm all the way through,
so I grow it back thicker than I need to.
That's the whole arrangement, and it's fair.
I have never once been cold in here.

[Verse 2]
Once a season, gentle hands,
a sound like scissors in a dream.
Everybody comes out lighter.
Everybody says it's clean.

[Chorus]
I never asked where it goes.
I never asked what it's worth.
I just liked being warm.
I just liked being warm.

[Verse 3]
Saw a cart go out past the fence last week,
piled up high and tied down tight.
Nobody mentioned it at supper.
Nobody mentions it at all.

And the shepherd smiled and held the gate
and thanked us for a good year,
and I thanked him back. I thanked him back.
That's the part I keep coming to.

[Chorus]
I never asked where it goes.
I never asked what it's worth.
I just liked being warm.
I just liked being warm.

[Bridge - slower, almost spoken]
It grows back. That's the thing.
It always grows back.
That's how you can take it every single year
and call it nothing at all.

[Outro - guitar and voice only]
I just liked being warm.
I just liked being warm.
```

---

## Notizen

* Kein einziger anklagender Satz. Die härteste Zeile ist **„and I thanked him
  back"** — er ist nicht empört, er ist beschämt.
* „It grows back" ist die Rechtfertigung, die er selbst benutzt. Die Bridge
  dreht sie um, ohne sie zu kommentieren.
* Der Track ist der Tiefpunkt von Akt I. Direkt danach kommt „Seven" — der
  Moment, in dem er zum ersten Mal etwas **macht**. Der Kontrast ist der
  Umschlagpunkt des Albums, also hier ruhig lang und schwer bleiben.
