# 03 · HEADCOUNT

*Akt I — Drinnen. Du bist keine Person, du bist ein Eintrag.
Der Refrain ist buchstäblich gezählt. Im letzten Vers fehlt eine Zahl —
niemand sagt etwas dazu.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Headcount
```

**Style of Music**
```
Mechanical industrial folk, 92 BPM, minor key. Rhythm built from stomps,
handclaps, chains, metal gates and a wooden clipboard, no kit drums. Low
male voice, flat and obedient, group chant answering. Counted numbers used
as the hook. Dry room, close mics, no reverb on the voice. Relentless,
orderly, physical. Sparse bass drone underneath.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
melodic pop, uplifting, orchestral, EDM, trap hi-hats, autotune, guitar solo,
warm strings, cheerful
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Intro - stomps and chains, numbers counted under the breath]
One. Two. Three. Four.

[Verse 1]
Stand on the line where the paint has worn.
Answer to the number they gave you when you were born.
Names are for the ones who visit.
Numbers are for us.

Hold still while the clipboard walks the row.
Say it clear, say it quick, and let it go.
It doesn't hurt. It doesn't take a minute.
It only takes the part of you that's in it.

[Chorus - counted, many voices]
One, two, three, the gate is closed.
Four, five, six, nobody's gone.
Seven hundred head and every single one
accounted for.
Say your number. Say it again.

[Verse 2]
Twice a day, the same soft voice.
Twice a day, the same small choice:
say it now, or say it slow,
either way the pencil goes.

I know my number better than my name.
I have said it more. That's all. That's the same.
Nobody minds. Nobody minds.
The counting keeps us safe.

[Chorus]
One, two, three, the gate is closed.
Four, five, six, nobody's gone.
Seven hundred head and every single one
accounted for.
Say your number. Say it again.

[Bridge - stripped, single voice, stomps only]
The counting says we're here.
The counting says we're here.
The counting says we're here.
Somebody has to say we're here.

[Final Chorus - starts full, thins out]
One, two, three, the gate is closed.
Four, five, six, nobody's gone.
Seven hundred head and every single one
accounted for.

[Outro - spoken, flat, no music]
Six hundred and ninety-nine.
```

---

## Notizen

* Der Schlusssatz ist der ganze Track. **Keine Betonung, keine Musik, kein
  Effekt.** Nur die Zahl, und sie stimmt nicht mehr.
* Niemand reagiert darauf — das ist der Punkt. Kein dramatischer Akkord.
* Wenn Suno die gezählten Zahlen nicht rhythmisch trifft, den Refrain als
  eigenen Take generieren und selbst einsetzen.
* Motivverbindung: Der Headcount kommt in Track 10 („Volunteers") zurück,
  dann sind es die Gezählten, die die Hufe heben.
