# 08 · GOOD DOG

*Akt II — Merken. Aus Sicht des Wachhundes.
Der wichtigste Track für die Glaubwürdigkeit des Albums: Der Gegner ist kein
Sadist, sondern loyal, stolz und überzeugt. Klingt wie eine Hymne — ist auch
eine, nur die falsche.*

---

## Suno

**Titel**
```
Good Dog
```

**Style of Music**
```
Warm military march meets folk anthem, 100 BPM, major key with minor turns.
Marching snare, bass drum, brass section, low strings, acoustic guitar.
Deep male lead vocal, honest, proud, unhurried, no menace in the tone.
Sincere and dignified, like a old service song. Full but not bombastic.
Ends quiet and bare.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
villain theme, horror, sinister, distorted, aggressive rap, trap, EDM,
sarcastic, comedic, autotune
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Verse 1]
I was born on this side of the fence,
same straw, same field, same rain as you.
Nobody asked the dog if he chose it.
They said good boy, and I was.

They taught me the rounds before I could count them.
I have walked this line eleven years.
I know the gate by the sound of the hinge.
I know all of you by name.

[Chorus]
Good dog. Good dog.
Head up, chest out, all night long.
Somebody has to stand at the gate,
and everybody sleeps because I do.
Good dog.

[Verse 2]
I have never bitten. Not once. Not ever.
The sound is usually enough.
I don't enjoy it. I'm not built for enjoying.
I'm built for standing still.

I know which of you is nervous.
I know which of you is sick.
I have carried three of you home in the dark
and none of you remember that.

[Chorus]
Good dog. Good dog.
Head up, chest out, all night long.
Somebody has to stand at the gate,
and everybody sleeps because I do.
Good dog.

[Verse 3]
I have seen the paint on the east wall.
I have seen the lamp on at two.
I'm not stupid. I'm just early.
I have all season. You have one night.

[Bridge - quiet, brass drops out, almost spoken]
And when they go, they never look at me.
Like I'm the fence. Like I'm the bell.
Like I'm a piece of what they're leaving.

I'm not the fence.
I'm the one who has to stay.

[Final Chorus - full, then collapsing to voice and drum]
Good dog. Good dog.
Head up, chest out, all night long.
Somebody has to stand at the gate.
Somebody has to stay.

[Outro - spoken, flat, warm, no music]
I know which one of you it is.
```

---

## Notizen

* **Nichts an diesem Track darf böse klingen.** Kein Moll-Effekt beim Outro,
  keine dunklen Streicher. Er ist ein guter Hund, er glaubt jedes Wort.
* Die Bridge ist der Kern: **„I'm not the fence. I'm the one who has to stay."**
  Wer geht, ist der Held. Wer bleibt, bleibt.
* Der Schlusssatz ist keine Drohung, sondern eine Feststellung — freundlich,
  fast traurig gesprochen. Er wird ihn nicht melden. Vielleicht.
* Wenn ein Track dieses Album über Protestlyrik hinaushebt, dann dieser.
  Beim Produzieren Zeit investieren.
