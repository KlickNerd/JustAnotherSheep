# 12 · THE OTHER SIDE ✅

*Akt IV — Raus. Die Entscheidung. Das Anthem des Albums.
**Text stammt von dir und ist unverändert.** Hier nur der Suno-Prompt und

## Suno

**Titel**
```
The Other Side
```

**Style of Music**
```
Anthemic alternative rock with folk roots, 120 BPM, minor verses opening into
a major chorus. Driving drums, distorted but warm guitars, prominent bass,
layered group vocals on the chorus. Male lead, gritty and urgent, full voice
in the chorus. Spoken distant choir in the intro. Big, wide, cinematic without
gloss. The one truly loud song on the record.
```

**Exclude Styles**
```
autotune, trap hi-hats, EDM drop, pop production, orchestral trailer, rap
verse, lo-fi, ambient
```

**Lyrics**
```
[Intro - spoken, distant choir, no drums]
Wake up. Fall in line.
Keep your head down. Everything is fine.

[Verse 1]
Same bell ringing every morning,
same old field beneath my feet.
Same voice telling us we're lucky,
while they're selling what we keep.

They say the fence is there to save us,
say the wolves are waiting near.
Funny how the ones who rule us
always need us full of fear.

[Pre-Chorus]
They call it peace, they call it home.
But every door says: Don't go.

[Chorus]
I'm just another sheep,
but I don't wanna sleep.
They tell me, "Stay inside,"
I wanna see the other side.

I'm just another sheep,
walking where the shadows lead.
Maybe freedom isn't safe,
but at least it belongs to me.

[Verse 2]
Outside, the city burns in color,
neon bleeding through the night.
Wolves and foxes, thieves and dreamers,
everyone decides their life.

No one tells me who to follow.
No one promises I'll win.
But I'd rather face the danger
than be safe beneath their skin.

[Bridge - beat drops out completely, voice and room only]
They said the gate was locked.
It wasn't.
They said the world was empty.
It wasn't.
They said I'd come back crawling.
Maybe.
But not tonight.

[Final Chorus - everything back in, full band, layered vocals]
I'm just another sheep,
but I finally woke from sleep.
They can keep their perfect lines,
I wanna see the other side.

I'm just another sheep,
no savior, no king, no prophecy.
I'm not lost.
I just stopped following.

[Outro - whispered, almost nothing]
Would you leave?
```

## Notizen

* **Die Bridge ist der stärkste Moment des ganzen Projekts.** Im Arrangement
  komplett nackt lassen: kein Beat, keine Gitarre, nur Stimme und Raum. Jede
  Antwort („It wasn't", „Maybe") mit einer echten Pause davor.
* Der Einsatz kommt hart aus dem abgebrochenen Wort von Track 11. Keine Pause
  zwischen den Tracks.
* Zwei Zeilen erklären etwas mehr als der Rest des Albums:
  *„Funny how the ones who rule us / always need us full of fear."*
  Funktioniert — falls du sie schärfen willst, wäre eine Alternative:
  *„They only ever lock the gate / from the side they're standing on."*
  Deine Entscheidung, beides ist richtig.
* Das geflüsterte „Would you leave?" ist an die Hörer gerichtet. So leise
  mischen, dass man kurz unsicher ist, ob man es gehört hat.
